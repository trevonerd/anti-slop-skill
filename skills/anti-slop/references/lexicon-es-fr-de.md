# Spanish, French and German lexicon

Shorter lists, built from community guides and editorial checklists, not from corpus counts. Reliability is one notch lower than the English and Italian files: treat every entry as **medium at most** unless it is chatbot residue. The structural signals (contrast, triads, empty openers, recap closers, missing details) carry over between languages; the exact words do not [ESS][ESH][FRD][FRV][DEB].

A native speaker should review copy in these languages before publishing. If you are not confident in the language, run AUDIT for facts, structure and formatting, and say clearly that the lexicon pass was limited.

## Spanish

| Signal | Examples | Reliability |
|---|---|---|
| Chatbot residue | "¡Claro! Aquí tienes...", "Espero que te sea útil", "¿Quieres que...?" [ESM] | strong (fingerprint) |
| Empty openers | "En el mundo actual", "Hoy en día" as the first words, "En la era digital", "Desde tiempos inmemoriales" [ESH][ESS] | medium |
| Importance prefaces | "Cabe destacar que", "Es importante señalar que", "Vale la pena mencionar" [ESH][ESS] | medium |
| Formula verbs | "juega un papel fundamental", "es la clave del éxito", "sin lugar a dudas" [ESH] | medium |
| Negative parallelism | "no solo... sino también", "No es X, sino Y", "No se trata de X, se trata de Y" [ESS][ESH] | medium to strong when repeated |
| Brochure words | crucial, fundamental, robusto, vibrante, panorama, ecosistema, potenciar, "sumergirse en", "un amplio abanico de", desbloquear [ESS][ESF] | weak alone |
| Recap closers | "En definitiva", "En resumen", "En conclusión" [ESH] | medium |
| Calques | "hacer sentido", "en base a", "aplicar para", "jugar un rol", "a nivel de" [ESS][ESH] | medium |
| Regional inconsistency | mixing voseo and tuteo, "ustedes" and "vosotros", or a placeless "neutral Spanish" [ESS] | medium |
| English typography | spaced em dash mid-sentence, missing "¿" and "¡", Title Case headings [ESM] | medium |

## French

| Signal | Examples | Reliability |
|---|---|---|
| Chatbot residue | "Bien sûr ! Voici...", "J'espère que cela vous aide" (by analogy with English and Italian residue; not from a counted corpus) | strong when present |
| Grand openers | "Dans un monde en constante évolution", "À l'ère du numérique", "Depuis la nuit des temps" [FRD][FRV] | medium |
| Emphasis formulas | "Il est important de noter que", "Il convient de souligner", "Force est de constater" [FRD][FRV] | medium |
| Chained connectors | "Par ailleurs", "En outre", "De plus" at the start of almost every paragraph [FRD] | medium |
| Balanced contrast | "Ce n'est pas seulement X, c'est aussi Y", "Loin d'être X, c'est Y", "non seulement... mais aussi" [FRD] | medium to strong when repeated |
| Emphasis vocabulary | enjeu majeur, levier, crucial, essentiel, incontournable, véritable, précieux, "plonger au cœur de", "naviguer dans" [FRD] | weak alone |
| Recap closers | "En somme", "En conclusion", "En définitive" [FRD] | medium |
| English typography | Title Case headings, straight quotes instead of « », no space before ":" ";" "!" "?", heavy em dashes, "Topic: promise" titles [FRD] | weak to medium (keyboards cause some of this) |

## German

| Signal | Examples | Reliability |
|---|---|---|
| Chatbot residue | "Gerne! Hier ist...", "Ich hoffe, das hilft" (by analogy with English and Italian residue; not from a counted corpus) | strong when present |
| Hinge phrases | "Es ist wichtig zu beachten", "In der heutigen schnelllebigen Welt", "Zusammenfassend lässt sich sagen" [DEB] | medium |
| Favourite words | vielfältig, entscheidend, nahtlos, "Landschaft" as a metaphor, "Reise", "im Bereich", ganzheitlich [DEB] | weak alone |
| Contrast template | "Es geht nicht nur um X, sondern auch um Y" [DEB] | medium to strong when repeated |
| Over-correct connectors | "Darüber hinaus", "folglich", "nichtsdestotrotz" in a row [DEB] | medium |
| Mandatory summary | final paragraph starting "Zusammenfassend" or "Insgesamt" that adds nothing [DEB] | medium |
