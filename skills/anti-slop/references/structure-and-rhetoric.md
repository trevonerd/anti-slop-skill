## Contrast and staging

| Signal | Examples | Reliability | Trend |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corrective contrast | "rather than" (4.3x), "not just X" (4.1x), "not simply" (157x in one 2026 model), "the _ is not simply" (576x), "is not just a _ it is" (153x in another family) [SLO][GRA] | strong in the long forms; "rather than" alone is weak | active, growing in one family [GRA] |
| "It's not X, it's Y" and "Not because X. Because Y." | Macaron found "isn't just Y, it's Z" 32 times in 600 model posts and never in 300 human posts [MAC][SLO] | strong | saturated, still frequent |
| Reversed form "Y rather than X" | [W1] | medium | active |
| "Less X, more Y" and "less like a _ and more like" | 82x and 105x in one family [GRA] | strong | active |
| Rhetorical question answered at once | "The result? A calmer morning.", "The lesson? ..." (10 times in 600 model posts, once in 300 human posts) [MAC][SLO] | strong in posts | active |
| Staged run-up | "Here's what most people miss:", "The part nobody talks about" [UNS] | medium to strong | active |
| Escalation | "But that's not all", "And it doesn't stop there" [SCR] | strong | saturated |

## Inflation

| Signal | Examples | Reliability | Trend |
|---|---|---|---|
| Importance and legacy | "a pivotal moment", "marks a shift", "reflects broader trends", "setting the stage for", "a testament to" [W1][UNS][TC] | strong in neutral prose | active |
| Trailing "-ing" analysis | "..., highlighting the importance of", "..., fostering a sense of community" [W1][UNS][TC] | strong | active |
| Free benefit | "without sacrificing / losing / compromising / requiring" [GRA] | medium to strong | active |
| Signalling that something matters | "matters because", "this distinction matters" [GRA][W1] | strong | active |
| Vague attribution | "experts say", "studies show", "many believe" [W1][UNS] | strong in informative text | active |
| Media-coverage insistence | "featured in leading outlets" [W1][UNS] | strong | active |
| Source disclaimers | "while specific details are limited", "should be treated as... rather than..." [W1] | strong | active |

## Rhythm

| Signal | Examples | Reliability | Trend |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compulsive rule of three | "calm, cozy, and timeless" | weak in explainers (human 23.6 vs model 23.8 per 10,000 words); medium in social posts (about 10-11 vs 6) [MAC]; Pangram 4x [PAN] | active. It counts when the three items repeat one idea [ECO] |
| Dramatic fragments | "Clear. Direct. Human." [UNS][SLO] | medium | Sloptells retires it as a cadence, an Italian guide still sees it in 2025-2026 models [HUM] |
| Metronome rhythm | every sentence about the same length; model sentence length varies much less [GRA][SLO] | medium (measurable over a paragraph, not over two lines) | active |
| Repeated openings | three sentences in a row starting the same way [UNS] | medium | active |
| False ranges | "from study sessions to soulful nights" with no real endpoints [UNS] | medium | active |

|---|---|---|---|
| Pseudo-agency | "the beat understands you", "this piano knows" [UNS] | medium | active |

## Document shape

| Signal | Examples | Reliability | Trend |
|---|---|---|---|
| Template structure | intro, three points, summary that repeats the intro; "Challenges" and "Future outlook" sections [W1][PANB] | medium | active |
| Sudden style change | one polished paragraph inside a sloppy text, or a switch between US and UK spelling [W1][UNS] | medium | active |
| Principle of balance | every argument followed by its counter-argument, a conclusion that refuses to decide [FRD][DEB] | medium | active |
| Over-literal answer to the brief | text repeats the exact words of the request and covers its points in the same order [FRD] | medium | active |

## Reading tips

- Look at paragraph shape as well as sentences. A contrast split across two sentences is still a contrast.
- Count across a series. One "Send this to someone who..." is fine; the same CTA in five Shorts is a template.
- Before flagging a list of three, ask whether the three items are different facts. Three instruments that are really in the track are information.
